FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in the setup fee?

The one-time $400 setup fee covers strategy, custom design, development, content population, SEO foundation, and launch. Think of it as getting a $5,000+ custom website for a fraction of the cost.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts or cancellation fees. You own your domain and content. If you cancel, you keep everything we've built — we'll provide a full export of your site.

How does the 14-day guarantee work?

If you're not satisfied with your website within 14 days of launch, we'll refund 100% of your monthly payment and setup fee. No questions, no hassle. We're that confident in our work.

What if I need more than 5 pages?

The Elite plan includes unlimited pages. For Essential and Pro plans, additional pages can be added for $50/page. Most local businesses do great with 3-7 pages.

How long does it take to build my site?

We deliver your first draft within 48 hours of receiving your business details. Revisions typically take 1-3 days. Most sites go live within 5-7 days from start to finish.

Do I need to provide content and images?

We'll help you every step of the way. If you have existing photos and text, great. If not, we can use stock imagery and write professional copy for your business at no extra cost.

Is the annual plan really cheaper?

Yes — annual billing gives you 2 months free compared to monthly billing. You pay for 10 months and get 12 months of service. Same features, same quality, just a better price.
