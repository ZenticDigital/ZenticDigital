Last updated: May 30, 2026

When you fill out our contact form, we collect your name, business name, city, email address, phone number, and any additional information you provide in your message. We also collect basic analytics data (page views, browser type, referral source) to improve our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

Your data is stored securely using industry-standard encryption. We use Formspree for form submissions and do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required by law.

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time. Contact us at hello@zenticdigital.com to exercise these rights.

6. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services such as Google Analytics and Formspree. These services have their own privacy policies governing data handling.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, email hello@zenticdigital.com.
